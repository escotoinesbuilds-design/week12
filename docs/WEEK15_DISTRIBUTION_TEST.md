## Anonymous responses

| Respondent | Understanding, in original language | Visual preference reported | Hypothetical click response |
|---|---|---|---|
| Person 1 | “It helps you find doctors depending on what you're looking for, like specialty, price and location, without having to search through a bunch of different websites.” | Preferred the simpler doctor-comparison graphic. | Would click if information were verified. |
| Person 2 | “It's a platform that helps people find doctors based on their preferences, budget and location.” | Preferred the graphic showing customizable preferences. | Would click because comparing nearby specialists would save time. |
| Person 3 | “Es una plataforma para buscar doctores privados y comparar opciones dependiendo de tu presupuesto, ubicación y especialidad.” | Preferred the doctor-comparison graphic with less text. | Would click if doctor information were trustworthy and verified. |
| Person 4 | “It helps people search for doctors and compare different options depending on their budget, location and preferences.” | Preferred stronger contrast and more visible information. | Would click but wanted a clearer booking process. |
| Person 5 | “It's an app that makes finding doctors easier by showing nearby options and letting you compare them based on your needs.” | Preferred the graphic emphasizing trustworthy information. | Would click and wanted access to doctor credentials. |

## Summary of reported reactions

- **5/5** described RutaSalud's basic value proposition as finding doctors based on needs or preferences and comparing options using criteria such as specialty, price/budget, location, or proximity.
- **5/5** stated hypothetical willingness to click, each with a stated condition, motivation, or requested clarification. This is not a count of actual clicks.
- Verification, trustworthiness, and credibility recurred: Persons 1 and 3 conditioned their stated click intention on verified/trustworthy information; Person 5 preferred a graphic emphasizing trustworthiness and requested credentials.
- Simpler comparison visuals were requested: Person 1 preferred a simpler doctor-comparison graphic; Person 3 preferred the doctor-comparison graphic with less text. Person 4 asked for stronger contrast and more visible information.
- Booking clarity remains unresolved: Person 4 wanted a clearer booking process. RutaSalud does not confirm or book appointments.
- Person 5's credentials request is feedback only. No credentials were collected or verified by this response summary.

These are descriptive counts from the five supplied notes, not a representative sample, measured usability result, or evidence that the launch page improved understanding.

### Summary and interpretation

- **5/5** reported completing the comparison task; these are self-reports, not independently observed results.
- Reported times ranged from approximately **1 to 3 minutes**; these are participant estimates, not measured durations.
- **5/5** reported recognizing that doctor information was simulated.
- **2/5** mentioned information overload (Persons 1 and 3).
- **1/5** mentioned booking confusion (Person 4).
- **2/5** reported no significant confusion (Persons 2 and 5).
- Reported next actions emphasized independent checks of real prices, availability, profiles, credentials, experience, affiliation, or references. These intentions do not verify any doctor information or show that follow-up occurred.

The self-reports suggest that the simulation disclosure was noticed, while result density and booking guidance remain concerns. They do not establish that the disclosure was effective for every user, that the live site or its analytics recorded visits, or that any person booked an appointment.
