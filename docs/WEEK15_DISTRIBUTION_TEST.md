# Week 15 qualitative audience responses

**Documentation date:** 2026-11-22

**Response collection date:** Not provided
**Source:** Five anonymous response summaries supplied by the project owner.

## Evidence limits

The five responses below are self-reported reactions and hypothetical click intentions. No collection date, recruitment method, outreach channel, consent record, participant demographics, task script, or observation record was supplied. The responses include preferences among graphic concepts, but the supplied notes do not establish whether the respondents saw the finished Week 15 assets or visited the launch page.

“Would click” records stated intent only. These notes do not establish actual clicks, website visits, impressions, conversions, completed tasks, engagement, or real-doctor verification. No such measures are inferred here.

| Respondent | Understanding, in original language | Visual preference reported | Hypothetical click response |

## Summary of reported reactions

- **5/5** described RutaSalud's basic value proposition as finding doctors based on needs or preferences and comparing options using criteria such as specialty, price/budget, location, or proximity.
- **5/5** stated hypothetical willingness to click, each with a stated condition, motivation, or requested clarification. This is not a count of actual clicks.
- Verification, trustworthiness, and credibility recurred: Persons 1 and 3 conditioned their stated click intention on verified/trustworthy information; Person 5 preferred a graphic emphasizing trustworthiness and requested credentials.
- Simpler comparison visuals were requested: Person 1 preferred a simpler doctor-comparison graphic; Person 3 preferred the doctor-comparison graphic with less text. Person 4 asked for stronger contrast and more visible information.
- Booking clarity remains unresolved: Person 4 wanted a clearer booking process. RutaSalud does not confirm or book appointments.
- Person 5's credentials request is feedback only. No credentials were collected or verified by this response summary.

These are descriptive counts from the five supplied notes, not a representative sample, measured usability result, or evidence that the launch page improved understanding.

## Evaluation of Week 15 brand decisions

| Existing decision | What these responses suggest | Evaluation and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **Clear, not clinical:** explain match status and next steps in direct, short language. | All five described the basic doctor-search/comparison idea in their own words. | **Calm and patient-led:** present options without pressure or choosing for the patient. | Person 2 preferred customizable preferences; the other responses focus on comparing options or information. | The patient-choice direction remains appropriate, but the supplied reactions do not directly measure whether the finished experience feels calm, independent, or non-pressuring. |
